# PURPOSE

The purpose of the AI Content Library is NOT storing videos.

The purpose is building an ever-growing business knowledge base.

Every imported content should increase the intelligence of the system.

Knowledge compounds over time.

Videos do not.

Every content item is knowledge.

Knowledge is reusable.

Knowledge becomes business assets.

Business assets generate revenue.

Never store raw content only.
