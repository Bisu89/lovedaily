# Purpose

This document defines how the company creates, delivers, and captures value.

Technology is not the business.

Software is not the business.

The business exists to help creators earn more while working less.

---

- Save time
- Build audiences

# Decision Framework

Every investment must answer:

Does it create reusable value?

Does it improve recurring revenue?

Does it reduce operational cost?

Does it strengthen the knowledge base?

Does it improve customer retention?

If the answer is mostly "No",

do not prioritize it.

# Long-Term Vision

The company should become the operating system for creator businesses.

Creators should rely on the platform every day.

The platform should become increasingly intelligent as more knowledge is collected.

Knowledge should become the company's strongest competitive advantage.

---

# Things We Will Not Do

We will not chase every trend.

We will not build unnecessary features.

We will not optimize vanity metrics.

We will not sacrifice long-term value for short-term attention.

We will not depend entirely on one platform.

---

# Final Principle

The business does not exist to ship software.

The business exists to create assets that continuously generate value.

Every product should strengthen the business.

Every customer interaction should strengthen the knowledge base.

Every piece of knowledge should improve future decisions.
