# CONTENT OPERATING SYSTEM

Version: 1.0

---

# PURPOSE

Content is not entertainment.

Content is the acquisition engine of the business.

Every piece of content should achieve at least one objective:

- Reach new people
- Increase followers
- Increase engagement
- Increase trust
- Bring users to our website
- Generate revenue
- Teach the AI system

If a piece of content achieves none of these,

it should not be produced.

---

# CONTENT PHILOSOPHY

We do not create content.

We build reusable business assets.

One piece of content should become:

- Facebook Video
- Story Database
- Website Article
- Newsletter
- Quote
- AI Training Data
- Digital Product
- Prompt Library

Never create content that can only be used once.

# HOOK FRAMEWORK

Good hooks create curiosity.

Examples

"I never expected..."

"She had no idea..."

"Wait until the ending..."

"This changed everything..."

"He cried when..."

Never use generic introductions.

Attention must be earned in the first seconds.

---

# STORY FRAMEWORK

Beginning

Introduce characters.

Introduce emotional context.

Middle

Create tension.

Increase uncertainty.

Reveal hidden information.

Ending

Deliver emotional payoff.

Leave one memorable lesson.

Never end abruptly.

---

# COMMENT FRAMEWORK

Every story should naturally invite discussion.

Avoid asking generic questions.

Instead ask emotionally meaningful questions.

Examples

Would you forgive them?

What would you do?

Who was right?

Would you react differently?

The purpose is conversation.

Not comments for the sake of comments.

# FINAL PRINCIPLE

The purpose of content is not views.
